Summary

One front door for every ticket sale

Today fans go from an Instagram or TikTok post to a bare Ticketmelon page. That page can take payment, but it can't tell the festival's story, answer the questions that stop people buying, or show you which posts and ads sold tickets.

We build [Event]'s own website: fast on mobile, easy for your team to edit, and reusable for every future edition. Fans pick their tickets and quantities on your site, then land on Ticketmelon with their order already filled in and pay with GCash, Maya or card. This is how Wonderfruit, one of Southeast Asia's best-known festivals, sells through Ticketmelon today.

What you get

Your own festival websiteLineup, tickets, schedule, venue and travel info, FAQ, past editions, news. Made for phones first.
Ticket bag → TicketmelonFans choose tiers and quantities on your site; checkout opens Ticketmelon with the order and promo code already in.
Easy editingChange prices, sold-out badges, lineup and FAQ yourself. Schedule lineup drops to go live at an exact time.
Know what sellsGoogle Analytics, Meta and TikTok tracking from first visit to checkout, plus a promo code per channel so you can see sales by source.
Your own fan listA waitlist and newsletter sign-up that you own, compliant with the Data Privacy Act.
Built for on-sale dayPages are served from a global network and won't crash when thousands of fans arrive at once.
Our recommendation: the Pro package (₱120,000). It is the smallest package that includes the editing system and the ticket bag, which a site meant to serve many editions needs.

Ticket money

How Ticketmelon billing works

Our fee is for building and running the website. Ticket money never passes through us. Fans pay Ticketmelon, and Ticketmelon pays you.

ItemWhat we found (to confirm with Ticketmelon)
Ticketmelon service fee (PH)About 3.99%–4% of the ticket price, plus ₱20 per ticket for tickets over ₱300
Payment processingAbout 3% for cards and e-wallets
Who pays the feesThe buyer, by default. You can choose to absorb part or all of it.
Setup or monthly feeNone. Free events cost nothing.
How fans payCredit/debit cards, GCash, Maya, GrabPay, ShopeePay
Payouts to youBank transfer. Published terms differ (on request in 5–10 business days, or within 14 business days after the event), so we will get your schedule in writing.
RefundsIf you refund, you pay 4% of the ticket price; Ticketmelon keeps its fees.

Example: one ₱3,000 general admission ticket

Ticket price (goes to you)₱3,000
Ticketmelon service fee (≈4% + ₱20)≈ ₱140
Payment processing (≈3%)≈ ₱94
Fan pays (if fees are passed on; VAT on fees extra)≈ ₱3,234

Figures are for illustration, based on Ticketmelon's last published Philippine rates. Ticketmelon has since taken down its public pricing page, so we will confirm your rates with your account manager before launch.

Performance option. If you prefer to share risk: build at 75% of list price, plus 4% of ticket revenue tracked to our links and promo codes, capped at one full build fee. Only available once Ticketmelon confirms per-code sales reporting.
